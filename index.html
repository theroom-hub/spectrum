How it works

Every order, from the counter to the download.

Each person in the lab does one simple thing. Spectrum keeps the order moving and the status true, on every computer and phone in the lab.

  1. 01

    Drop-off

    The order is created at the counter and paid on a card terminal connected to Spectrum. A label with a QR code is printed for every roll.

  2. 02

    Develop

    The developer scans the QR with a phone camera and taps Developed. No app to install.

  3. 03

    Scan

    The scan operator scans the same QR, reads the client notes and taps Start scanning. The scan folder is created and named from the order itself, with client and project, so nothing is ever typed or misspelled.

  4. 04

    Automatic intake

    When scanning is done, the operator just moves the roll folders made by the scanner into the created project folder. Spectrum does the rest: it removes system files, renames every roll and frame and turns each image upright with its built-in AI. The clean folder is marked as scanned and ready for its final review.

  5. 05

    Ready

    Once the operator has reviewed and approved the scans, the download link is created automatically, the order turns Ready and the client email is drafted, waiting for one click to send.

  6. 06

    Collected

    Hand over the negatives. Shelf, payment and history stay on record.

Scans

Scans that sort themselves.

The scan operator approves the scans and goes straight on to the next order. Spectrum's built-in AI takes over: it turns every frame upright, with 97% accuracy in our tests on real Noritsu scans, renames every roll and frame, removes system files and prepares a clean copy, ready to work on.

Named for people

Scanner file names become client, project, roll and frame. System files such as Info_HD.txt and .DS_Store are left out.

Every frame upright

An AI model running on your Mac, trained on film photography, turns frames. In our test on real Noritsu scans it placed 97 of 100 frames correctly.

Pixels never touched

Frames are turned the way Adobe Bridge does it, in the metadata. Files are copied byte for byte: no recompression, no colour profile changes.

Complete or not at all

A project folder appears only once it is fully copied, so nobody starts retouching half a delivery. The copy stays untouched.

Delivery and payments

Ready means ready: link, email, invoice.

Spectrum publishes a ready project on your own download page, marks the order Ready and drafts the email. You read it and press Send.

Your own download page

Under your lab's name, no third-party transfer service. Links close after 30 days, an optional password, and you see when the client downloaded.

Card payments from the order

Charge on your terminal straight from the order. The payment is recorded by itself, with no retyping.

Invoices without paperwork

A numbered invoice is issued when the order is paid in full, as a print-ready PDF in your lab's details.

Revenue you can trust

Money received by month and by method, and a cash book export for your accountant.

Your data

Your clients stay in your lab.

Runs in your lab

Clients, orders, prices and payments live on your own computer, not in someone else's cloud.

One small door to the outside

Clients reach only a separate delivery area holding copies of their files. Nothing else in Spectrum can be reached from the internet.

Keys kept local

Card terminal keys and the mail password are stored only on your computer.

AI that stays home

The model that turns frames runs on your Mac. Your clients' images are never sent away to be analysed.
